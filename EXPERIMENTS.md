# Experimental Log

Aggregated record of the experiments behind the project report. Each hypothesis lists the runs, their
settings, and their final held-out results. Raw per-epoch logs live in `output/<run>/logs/runs.csv`
(regenerated by running training); the figures are regenerated by `scripts/report_figures.py` and the
statistics by `scripts/stats_tests.py`, `scripts/stats_h2.py`, `scripts/detection_f1.py`, and
`scripts/cellpose_baseline.py`.

## H1 — Does self-supervision help when labels are scarce?

**Run:** `output/H1.v9`. Pretrain JEPA on the recording (labels removed) → fine-tune vs. from-scratch
at each labeled fraction.

**Pretraining** (`jepa_pretrained_h1`, 9 epochs): training loss falls 1.47 → 0.32 while held-out loss
rises 4.9 → 7.3 — the objective overfits (does not generalize).

**Held-out test scores** (per-clip, 128²):

| Labeled fraction | SSL Dice | SSL mIoU | From-scratch Dice | From-scratch mIoU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10%  | 0.626 | 0.675 | **0.703** | **0.730** |
| 50%  | 0.839 | 0.846 | **0.860** | **0.868** |
| 75%  | 0.868 | 0.874 | **0.887** | **0.895** |
| 100% | **0.895** | **0.903** | 0.878 | 0.885 |

**Statistics** (paired Wilcoxon, n = 120 clips): significant at both fractions tested, but the sign
flips — SSL **+0.017** Dice at 100% (p ≈ 4e-20) yet **−0.077** at 10% (p ≈ 3e-21). No robust
sample-efficiency advantage; SSL is worst where it was expected to help most.

## H2 — Does self-supervision transfer across species?

**Run:** `output/H2.v7`. Pretrain JEPA on non-mouse source (Drosophila + zebrafish, ~43k frames /
20 recordings, unlabeled) → fine-tune on the mouse target at each fraction. Leakage-free by
construction.

**Held-out mouse test scores:**

| Labeled fraction | Cross-species SSL Dice | Cross-species SSL mIoU | From-scratch Dice | From-scratch mIoU |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10%  | 0.639 | 0.678 | **0.698** | **0.727** |
| 50%  | 0.818 | 0.826 | **0.849** | **0.858** |
| 75%  | 0.858 | 0.863 | **0.886** | **0.894** |
| 100% | **0.884** | **0.892** | 0.873 | 0.881 |

**Statistics** (paired Wilcoxon): cross-species vs. same-species SSL = **−0.011** Dice (p ≈ 5e-21) — a
small, statistically significant but practically negligible penalty; cross-species vs. from-scratch =
**+0.006** (p ≈ 1e-10). Cross-species transfer is on par with training from scratch.

## H3 — Are the learned features stable and distinct per neuron?

**Run:** `output/H3_v9`. Per-neuron embeddings pooled over each footprint; within-neuron (same neuron
across frames) vs. between-neuron (different neurons, same frame) cosine similarity.

| Encoder | Within | Between | Gap (within − between) | Cohen's d |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Untrained (random init) | 0.978 | 0.972 | 0.007 | 0.16 |
| Cross-species SSL | 0.968 | 0.940 | 0.028 | — |
| Same-species SSL | 0.875 | 0.818 | 0.057 | 0.66 |
| Supervised | 0.793 | 0.585 | **0.208** | 1.45 |

**Statistics** (Mann-Whitney, within > between): significant for every encoder (all p ≈ 0). Effect size
orders them: negligible (untrained, d = 0.16), medium (self-supervised, d = 0.66), large (supervised,
d = 1.45). The supervised encoder is trained on the very footprints that define the pooling, so its
gap is a near-tautological upper reference.

## Field-level evaluation and Cellpose comparison

Native-resolution (512²) instance evaluation against the full footprint (330 neurons). Detection F1
matches a predicted component to a ground-truth neuron at IoU ≥ 0.5.

| Model | Dice | Detection F1 | Precision | Recall | Instances recovered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| JEPA fine-tune (100%) | 0.700 | 0.264 | 0.413 | 0.194 | 155 |
| From scratch (100%) | **0.711** | **0.279** | 0.433 | 0.206 | 157 |
| Cellpose (zero-shot) | 0.279 | 0.170 | 0.352 | 0.112 | 105 |

Every detection F1 is low: the segmenter covers the right pixels but merges adjacent somata, recovering
only ~155 of 330 neurons. The "beats Cellpose" gap is not a fair comparison (my models are trained on
the target; Cellpose is zero-shot with automatic diameter estimation).

---

## Summary of findings

- **H1:** self-supervision does not beat from-scratch; the difference flips sign and is worst at 10%.
- **H2:** cross-species representations transfer to mouse with no meaningful penalty (on par with scratch).
- **H3:** self-supervision separates neurons in time above chance-level structure; supervised is strongest.
- **Across all:** good pixel Dice, low instance detection F1 (~0.2) — neurons merge rather than separate.
- **Root cause:** the self-supervised objective overfits (train loss down, held-out loss up).
